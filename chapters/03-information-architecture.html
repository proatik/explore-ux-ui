Information Architecture is the structure underneath every screen.

Menus, tabs, breadcrumbs

Sitemaps: depth vs breadth

A sitemap is the IA blueprint. Tap sections to expand.

    🪜 Deep & narrow

    3 options × 4 levels
    4 taps to reach anything
    More chances to take a wrong turn

    🌳 Broad & shallow

    9 options × 2 levels
    2 taps to reach anything
    More to scan per screen
    • Both hold the same 81 items.
    • Research usually favors broad and shallow, if groups are clear.
    • The "3-click rule" is a myth. Users keep clicking if each step feels right (information scent).

      🎯 Key takeaways

      • Structure and labels decide findability, not visuals.
      • Use users' words; validate with a card sort (build) and a tree test (check).
      • Every branch in a user flow is work to build and test. Ask for the edge cases.
