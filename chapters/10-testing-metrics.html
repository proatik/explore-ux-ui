Think aloud

Don't rescue

  • Silence is data
  • Answer questions with "What would you expect?"

Watch, don't ask

  • What users do beats what they say
  • Note hesitations, not just failures

Common A/B pitfalls

👀
PeekingStopping when it "looks significant" inflates false winners.
🎰
Too many variantsTest 20 versions and one will "win" by chance.
✨
Novelty effectNew things get clicks at first. Run full weeks.
⚖️
Sample ratio mismatchPlanned 50/50 but got 52/48? Something's broken. Don't trust the result.
🐜
Too little trafficSmall sites can't detect small lifts. Use qualitative research instead.
🎯
No primary metricPick one success metric before launch, plus guardrail metrics.

🎯 Key takeaways

  • Analytics shows what happens. Usability testing shows why.
  • Define the success metric before you ship, not after.
  • Plan an A/B test's sample size upfront, and don't peek.
