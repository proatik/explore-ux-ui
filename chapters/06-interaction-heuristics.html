Fitts's law: tap the targets

Tap the black dot, then the purple target, as fast as you can. 9 rounds.

Your results

Each dot is one tap. Harder (smaller + farther) → slower.

Average time by size

  • Time grows with distance, shrinks with size
  • Primary buttons: big and close to the thumb
  • Screen edges and corners are easy targets

🎯 Key takeaways

  • Bigger and closer is faster (Fitts). Fewer choices is faster (Hick).
  • The 10 heuristics turn "I don't like it" into specific, fixable feedback.
  • Every action needs feedback. Anything over ~1s needs a visible indicator.
