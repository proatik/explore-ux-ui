Say ≠ Do

The #1 rule of research. Combine both kinds of method to see the full picture.

🗣️ THEY SAY"I always compare prices."
👀 THEY DOReorder the same place every time.
🗣️ THEY SAY"I'd pay for faster delivery."
👀 THEY DOPick the free, slower option.
🗣️ THEY SAY"The app is easy to use."
👀 THEY DOTake 4 tries to find order history.

🎯 Key takeaways

  • Watch what users do, not just what they say.
  • Ask about past behavior, never future wishes or features.
  • Qual finds the why (5 users), quant measures how many (hundreds). You need both.
