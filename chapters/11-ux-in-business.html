🧾 Design debt

  • 5 button styles where 1 should exist
  • One-off colors and spacing
  • Old patterns nobody updated
  • Track it in the backlog, like tech debt
  • Budget a slice of each sprint to pay it down
  • Prevent it with a design system (Chapter 08)
