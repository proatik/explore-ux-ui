      💡 Learn · Color
      
The 60-30-10 rule

      
60% dominant, 30% secondary, 10% accent. The accent is rare, so it pulls the eye to the action.

      

        

        

          
 60% Dominant backgrounds

          
 30% Secondary sidebar, cards

          
 10% Accent buttons, highlights

      💡 Learn · Space, Grid & Gestalt
      
The 8-point spacing system

      
All spacing uses multiples of 8 (4 for tight spots). Fewer choices, consistent rhythm, easier handoff.
