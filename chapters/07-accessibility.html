POUR: the 4 principles of WCAG

      
Every WCAG rule falls under one of these.

      


      
How much is "accessible enough"?

      
WCAG has three conformance levels. Laws and contracts point to AA.

      

        

          
A
Minimum. Without it, some users are completely blocked.

          
AA
🎯 YOUR DEFAULT TARGET
Standard. Includes all of A. Named by most laws, procurement and contracts.

          
AAA
Enhanced. Great for specific flows. Not expected site-wide.

          
Current version: WCAG 2.2 (2023). Many laws still cite 2.1 AA. 2.2 AA covers it.

        

        

          
🇺🇸 ADA & Section 508
Website lawsuits are common. Courts and the DOJ point to WCAG AA.

          
🇪🇺 European Accessibility Act
Enforced since 28 June 2025: e-commerce, banking, transport, e-books. Standard: EN 301 549 (→ WCAG AA).

          
🇬🇧 Equality Act & public sector rules
Public sector sites must meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

          
Not legal advice. Ask legal which standard your contracts name.
